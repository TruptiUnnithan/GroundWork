The task playbook

Seven steps for every task.

All seven. Full brief in writing.
  1. 1

    Capture the ask

    Exact words, who, when, deadline. Don't interpret yet.

    Skip
  2. 2

    Find the why

    What does it feed? Who cares? What's good, by when?

    Skip
  3. 3

    Map what exists

    Search Copilot and ADO. Note the names that keep appearing.

    Skip
  4. 4

    Talk to two people

    One who knows the history, one who'll feel the outcome.

    Skip
  5. 5

    Send the brief

    “Here's how I'm reading this. Anything wrong?”

    SkipFive lines in chat
  6. 6

    Share at 30%

    Early feedback is cheap. Late feedback is a rewrite.

    Skip
  7. 7

    Close the loop

    Two-line summary. Log the win. Update Glossary and People.

    Skip

Stuck more than half a day? Add it to the Questions bank and ask. Silence is the failure.

The one-page brief

  1. The ask, in their words
  2. Requester and date
  3. Why it matters
  4. What done looks like
  5. Deadline: hard or soft
  6. Out of scope
  7. People and their stake
  8. Prior work found
  9. Risks and unknowns
  10. First check-in date
